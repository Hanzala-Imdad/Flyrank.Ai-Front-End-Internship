## Pressure-Test Results

Claude identified the following key issues with the sitemap:

1. **Claim is too vague** — "AI-powered" needs concrete proof, not just words
2. **No social proof** — no testimonials, no live URLs, no signal a real human worked with me
3. **Contact page is weak** — needs direct email + LinkedIn, not just a form
4. **Weakest point** — the credibility gap between the claim on the homepage and the projects that must prove it

### One Thing I Will Change

> *"I need at least one project that proves AI integration at a technical level, one that proves clean modern frontend, and one that proves startup-ready thinking — before the portfolio goes live."*
